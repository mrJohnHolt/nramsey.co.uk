## Design
- Modern, premium, clean, understated. Purple is the accent, not the whole identity.
- Palette: Primary Purple #5B2C83, Dark Purple #3D1D5C, Soft Lavender #EEE8F3, Off-White #FAF9FB, Charcoal #252525, Medium Grey #666666, White #FFFFFF. Verify against the logo.
- Fonts: serif display plus clean sans (Manrope, DM Sans or Plus Jakarta Sans). Not Inter. Pairing to be confirmed.
- Icons for the four pillars: Font Awesome free.
- No calculators, handshake or generic finance imagery.
- About three authentic photos of Nigel: hero (landscape, natural light, space for headline), About (relaxed, seated or in conversation), optional third (real meeting or Yorkshire/Lancashire context).
- Subtle motion only.
