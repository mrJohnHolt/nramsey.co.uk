Whatever the situation, experienced Finance Director support can help you understand what's happening, identify what needs attention and make better decisions.

A Finance Director who gets involved

I'm Nigel Ramsey. I've spent more than 25 years in senior finance roles across manufacturing, FMCG, brewing, hospitality and retail. I've worked with owners, MDs, operational teams and senior executives through growth, acquisition, integration, restructuring, investment and change.

My approach is straightforward: understand the business, understand the numbers, find the issues, fix what needs fixing and help make better decisions.

Finance doesn't operate in isolation. The numbers are a reflection of what is happening across the wider business: operations, sales, people, customers, suppliers and strategy.
